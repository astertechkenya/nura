Privacy Policy

Your information

What we collect when you shop with NURA, why we need it, who else handles it, and how to have it changed or deleted.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

NURA is an online fashion shop based in Nairobi, Kenya. For anything in this policy, contact us at astertechkenya@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +254 705 439 631.

What we collect, and why

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. We need these to deliver your order, send you its confirmation and updates, and reach you about it.
  • When you create an account: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; nobody at NURA can read it.
  • Your cart and wishlist, so they’re there when you come back.
  • If you join the newsletter: your email address, only after you confirm it from the email we send. Every newsletter has a link to unsubscribe.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic browser details, used to keep the shop secure (for example, to slow down someone trying many passwords) and to find and fix errors.

Payments

M-Pesa payments are processed by Safaricom: we send them your phone number and the amount, and they tell us whether it was paid, with the M-Pesa receipt number. Card payments are made on Paystack’s secure page: your card number never reaches NURA. We only receive the result and the last four digits of the card.

Who else handles your information

We use trusted services to run the shop. They handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Safaricom (M-Pesa) and Paystack (cards), for payments;
  • Resend, to send order and account emails;
  • Netlify and Render, which host the website and its server, and Neon, which hosts our database;
  • Sentry, which tells us when something breaks, set up not to collect your personal details.

Some of these services store information outside Kenya. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in and holds your cart. It’s essential for the shop to work. Signed out, your wishlist is kept in your own browser. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax. A sign-in lasts up to 30 days. If you delete your account, your account, cart, wishlist and newsletter subscription are removed at once; past orders are kept for our records but no longer linked to an account.

Your rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it. You can delete your account yourself under My account, or write to us. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
